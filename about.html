About

I am a digital artist.

My practice revolves around the intersection of code, data bending, and digital decay. I am particularly fascinated by generative art writing rules for machines only to intentionally disrupt them, creating a dialogue between structured code and chaotic corruption. While these experiments begin quietly on my desktop, I’ve been incredibly fortunate to see my procedural pieces and fragmented pixels scale up at public locations and art exhibitions around the world.

I'm deeply grateful to the community and collectors who support my digital journey.
