Events

Physical exhibitions, digital festivals, and public screen takeovers across North America, Europe, and Asia: [1], [2]

October 2023: XCOLLABZ Cyberpunk 2023

Location: TSX Entertainment Billboard, Times Square, New York City, United States

Details: Organised by the digital art collective XCollabz, Cruel's glitch aesthetic was broadcast live onto the massive, high-definition TSX Times Square Screen in the heart of Manhattan. [1]

February 2024: Threads of Connection

Location: NFTgoat Gallery, Toronto, Canada

Details: Curated by the arts organisation Crown & Palette, this physical-meets-digital gallery exhibition featured Cruel alongside a global roster of 55 digital artists showcasing narrative digital art and contemporary crypto-art.

28–30 May 2024: Art of Tezos: In Motion & Art Beat

Location: Non Fungible Conference (NFC Summit), Pavilhão Carlos Lopes, Lisbon, Portugal

Details: Co-curated by XCollabz and Objkt, Cruel's moving-image glitch artworks were streamed on the NFC Summit Main Stage and featured during dedicated immersive art sessions. [1]

25 September – 4 October 2024: PHŌS_ Nuestro Fuego (NXT Festival)

Location: Centro Andaluz de Arte Contemporáneo (CAAC), Monasterio de la Cartuja, Seville, Spain

Details: Presented by El Faro and supported by the Tezos Foundation, this museum-grade new media exhibition explored light, code, and digital rituals. Cruel was showcased alongside prominent digital figures such as Agoria and Kim Asendorf. [1]

September 2024: Artcrush Public Screen Takeover

Location: Digital Out-Of-Home (DOOH) Billboards, Jakarta, Indonesia

Details: Facilitated by Artcrush Gallery, Cruel’s chromatic-shifted compositions replaced standard commercial advertisements on massive urban LED screens across high-traffic transit corridors. [1], [2]
